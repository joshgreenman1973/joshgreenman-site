Josh Greenman

I write about New York City: its politics, its policy and how it’s governed.

I’m managing editor of Vital City, a journal of ideas about New York policy. For nearly 17 years I edited and wrote opinion pieces at the New York Daily News, as op-ed editor and then editorial page editor.

More

Before that, speechwriting and policy in New York and Washington. Years ago I wrote headlines for The Onion. I publish Thoughts About Stuff on Substack. I live in Brooklyn with my wife and our two daughters.

Lately I’ve been making data tools about New York too. They’re mixed in with the writing.
